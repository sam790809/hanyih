關於漢益塑膠About Han Yih Plastics

漢益塑膠實業股份有限公司創立於 1984 年，深耕真空成型與塑膠加工領域逾四十載。我們貫徹「專業與誠信」的精神，通過 ISO 9001 國際品質管理體系認證， 以標準化流程兼顧高品質與高效率產能。我們的合作夥伴涵蓋多家上市上櫃知名企業，同時亦彈性服務廣大的中小型企業與新創品牌，成為海內外客戶深受信賴的指定夥伴。 為提供最具競爭力的服務，廠內常備齊全的各式塑料原料，並提供低起訂量（MOQ）的彈性合作模式；無論是小量樣品試產、大規模量產或進出口貿易，漢益塑膠皆能為您提供最精準可靠的一站式塑膠加工解決方案。 Founded in 1984, Han Yih Plastic Co., Ltd. brings over four decades of expertise to vacuum forming and custom plastic processing. Upholding our core principles of "Professionalism and Integrity", we are ISO 9001 certified and utilize standardized workflows to achieve high quality and efficient capacity. Our clientele spans publicly listed corporations as well as agile small-to-medium enterprises and startups, making us a trusted partner both domestically and globally. To deliver maximum competitive value, we maintain a comprehensive raw material inventory and support flexible low Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ). Whether for small-batch trial runs, high-volume mass production, or international trade, Han Yih Plastic delivers the most reliable end-to-end custom plastic processing solutions.

品質與環境認證Quality & Certification

ISO 9001:2015 國際品質管理認證ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System Certification

漢益全廠通過 ISO 9001:2015 品質管理系統驗證，從原物料進料檢驗、真空成型加工、後製程沖切到最終成品檢驗，皆落實嚴格的品質監控。 Han Yih is fully certified under ISO 9001:2015. Strict quality inspection is executed at every stage, from incoming raw material verification and thermoforming to die-cutting and final package delivery.

公司發展沿革Company History Timeline

1984

創立嘉元有限公司，資本額新台幣 50 萬元。Established Jiayuan Co., Ltd. with a capital of NT$ 500,000.

1989

更名為漢益塑膠有限公司，增資至 500 萬元，A 棟新廠落成。Renamed to Han Yih Plastics Co., Ltd., increased capital to 5 Million NTD, and completed Building A plant.

1990

進入手工具市場，陸續爭取到台灣工具界五大廠訂單，成為業界主要供應商之一。Entered the hand tool packaging market and became a key supplier to Taiwan's top hand tool manufacturers.

1998

跨足高科技電子產業包裝領域。Expanded business into high-tech electronic component packaging.

2003

B 棟（二層）廠房擴建完成，大幅提升產能。Completed Building B expansion to significantly boost production capacity.

2015

C 棟（三層）廠房擴建完成。Completed 3-story Building C expansion.

2017

增資至 2000 萬元，新建倉庫並引進全新設備。Capital increased to 20 Million NTD; built a new warehouse and upgraded machinery.

2022

D 棟擴建完成，設立百萬級潔淨室進行環境監控管理，確保頂級生產品質；同年更名為「漢益塑膠實業股份有限公司」。 Completed Building D expansion and built a Class 1,000,000 cleanroom for high-precision manufacturing.

2024

成立專業模具部門，專注於鋁合金、電木、EVA泡棉等材料加工，提供高品質真空成型模、高週波模、熱封模具與治具之設計製造。 Established a specialized tooling department focusing on the processing of materials such as aluminum alloy, Bakelite, and EVA foam, providing high-quality design and manufacturing for vacuum forming molds, high-frequency dies, heat sealing molds, and fixtures.

2025

導入高性能進口成型機、高精度裁斷機與自動化機械手臂，全面提升生產效率與產品品質，為客戶提供更優質的升級服務。 Integrating high-performance imported thermoforming machines, high-precision die-cutting systems, and robotic automation to elevate production efficiency and quality, delivering superior upgraded solutions to our clients.
