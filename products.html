真空成型泡殼與 Tray 盤產品總覽Thermoformed Blisters & Trays Overview

真空成型泡殼名稱繁多（如塑膠盒、Tray盤、脆盤、托盤、泡殼、內襯、真空成型盒、吸塑），依使用場景可分為「工廠周轉類」、「賣場零售包裝」與「產品設備夾層外殼」三大類： Thermoformed plastic packaging (trays, blisters, clamshells, inserts, Vacuum Forming, Thermoforming) is categorized into three main applications: Industrial Transport , In-Plant Trays and Retail Display Packaging and Product & Equipment Interlayer /Housings.

一、 工廠廠內與廠際周轉類 (工業與電子類)1. In-Plant & Inter-Factory Transport Trays (Industrial & Electronics)

此類產品主要用於保護精密零件在製程與運輸過程中不撞傷，或配合自動化設備進行作業： Designed to protect delicate parts from damage during transport and seamlessly interface with automated assembly lines:

週轉 Tray 盤Anti-Static (ESD) / Conductive Trays

使用時通常會旋轉 180 度交叉堆疊，此類產品主要用於保護精密零件在製程與運輸過程中不撞傷。 Features 180-degree stackable design.Designed to protect delicate parts from damage during transport.

抗靜電/導電 Tray 盤Anti-Static (ESD) / Conductive Trays

選用 ESD 抗靜電或導電級材料（PS, ABS, PET, PP），精準控制表面電阻，防止靜電放電損害靜電敏感電子元件 (ESD-sensitive devices)。 Made from anti-static or conductive PS, ABS, PET or PP materials. Controls surface resistivity to protect ESD-sensitive components.

自動化 Tray 盤Automated Line Trays

高精度尺寸控制，專為配合客戶廠內的自動化機械手臂與組裝設備設計，具備同向堆疊定位功能，可配合感測器及視覺定位。 High-precision tray designed for accurate robotic positioning, sensor detection, and machine vision alignment.

精密零件保護Protective Covers for Precision Parts

用於烤漆、電鍍、陽極或精密加工後的零件表面防塵、防刮與防撞保護。 Provides dustproof, scratch-resistant, and impact protection for painted, plated, or anodized parts.

插式泡殼Slot-in Blisters

插式設計適用於薄片狀產品（如玻璃或 PCB 板） Slot-in designs for thin substrate glass or PCBs.

對翻盒Turnover Trays

對翻盒則專為製程中與相對應治具快速翻轉轉移產品而設計。 Turnover trays for quick flipping and transferring of workpieces.

三、產品設備夾層外殼類3. Product & Equipment Interlayer /Housings

為工業機器、醫療設備、電子儀器與器材設備量身打造的外殼或夾層。採用 ABS、HIPS、PMMA(壓克力) 或 PP 等高強度、耐衝擊厚板材料，透過 CNC 精密切削模具與精密後加工成型。 Custom-designed housings and interlayers tailored for industrial machinery, medical devices, electronic instruments, and equipment. Manufactured using high-impact , heavy-gauge sheet materials such as ABS, HIPS, PMMA (Acrylic), or PP, formed through CNC precision-milled molds and precision secondary finishing.

  • 常用材質：Materials: ABS（耐衝擊/防火等級）、HIPS、PMMA(壓克力)或 PP 。 ABS (Impact-resistant/Flame-retardant), HIPS, PMMA ,PP.
  • 技術優勢：Advantages: 開模成本遠低於鋼模射出、結構強度高、可進行多軸 CNC 銑削加工與表面噴漆/紋路處理。 significantly lower tooling costs than injection molding, robust structural strength, multi-axis CNC secondary machining, and custom surface finishing/painting.
