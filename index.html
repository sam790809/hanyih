真空成型泡殼與 Tray 盤產品總覽Thermoformed Blisters & Trays Overview

真空成型泡殼名稱繁多（如塑膠盒、Tray盤、脆盤、泡殼、內襯、真空成型盒），依使用場景可分為「工廠廠內周轉」與「賣場零售包裝」兩大類： Thermoformed plastic packaging (trays, blisters, clamshells, inserts) is categorized into two main applications: Industrial Transport & In-Plant Trays and Retail Display Packaging.

一、 工廠廠內與廠際周轉類 (工業與電子類)1. In-Plant & Inter-Factory Transport Trays (Industrial & Electronics)

此類產品主要用於保護精密零件在製程與運輸過程中不撞傷，或配合自動化設備進行作業： Designed to protect delicate parts from damage during transport and seamlessly interface with automated assembly lines:

抗靜電/導電 Tray 盤Anti-Static (ESD) / Conductive Trays

選用 ESD 抗靜電或導電級材料（PS, ABS, PET），精準控制表面電阻，防止靜電放電損害靜電敏感電子元件 (ESD-sensitive devices)。使用時通常會旋轉 180 度交叉堆疊。 Made from anti-static or conductive PS, ABS, or PET materials. Controls surface resistivity to protect ESD-sensitive components. Features 180-degree stackable design.

自動化 Tray 盤Automated Line Trays

高精度尺寸控制，專為配合客戶廠內的自動化機械手臂與組裝設備設計，具備同向堆疊定位功能。 Precision dimensions tailored for robotic arms and automated pick-and-place manufacturing systems.

精密零件保護蓋Protective Covers for Precision Parts

用於烤漆、電鍍、陽極或精密加工後的零件表面防塵、防刮與防撞保護。 Provides dustproof, scratch-resistant, and impact protection for painted, plated, or anodized parts.

插式泡殼 / 對翻盒Slot-in Blisters / Turnover Trays

插式設計適用於薄片狀產品（如玻璃或 PCB 板）；對翻盒則專為製程中與相對應治具快速翻轉轉移產品而設計。 Slot-in designs for thin substrate glass or PCBs; turnover trays for quick flipping and transferring of workpieces.

二、 賣場零售展示包裝類2. Retail Display Packaging

真空成型原理與技術解析Thermoforming Process & Mold Engineering

真空成型原理Thermoforming Process Principle

真空成型係將熱塑性塑膠板材或卷料加熱軟化後，將模具與材料間抽真空，利用大氣壓力使材料緊密服貼於模具表面，隨後經由風冷或水冷冷卻定型，最後吹氣脫模，即完成初步成型。 Thermoforming involves heating a thermoplastic sheet until pliable, then drawing a vacuum between the sheet and the mold so atmospheric pressure forces the sheet onto the mold surface. Once cooled by air or water, air is blown to release the finished part.

成型後需進行後加工及裁切，常見如：沖床沖壓（適用於立體造型邊緣）、雷射刀模沖切（裁切平面外框與蝴蝶洞）、熱封/高週波黏合、網版印刷與折邊加工。 Post-processing options include hydraulic press trimming, steel rule die cutting, heat/high-frequency sealing, silk-screen printing, and edge folding.

真空成型特性說明Technical Characteristics

  • 厚度變化Thickness Variation： 材料經拉伸後，各處厚度不均勻，拉伸高度越高之處壁厚越薄，最大厚度不超過原始板材厚度。 Sheet stretching causes non-uniform wall thickness; deeper draws result in thinner walls.
  • 轉角 R 角Corner Radius (R-Corner)： 受到材料覆蓋與拉伸限制，造型轉折處必然呈現 R 角，無法做到極致尖角。 Material stretch constraints necessitate rounded corners rather than sharp 90-degree angles.
  • 開發成本優勢Cost-Effective Tooling： 相較於射出成型模具，真空成型模具開發費用極低，特別適合中大量及客製化生產。 Tooling costs are significantly lower than injection molds, making it ideal for medium-to-large custom runs.

真空成型模具製造工藝Mold Tooling Technologies

CNC 精密切削鋁模CNC Machined Aluminum Molds

直接採用一整塊高品質鋁合金原料，透過 CNC 數值控制铣床直接切削成型。優點為尺寸精度極高、表面光滑、風路設計精細，適合高精度的電子 Tray 盤與醫療包裝模具。 Directly carved from solid high-grade aluminum blocks via CNC milling. Provides superior dimensional accuracy, smooth surface finish, and optimized vacuum venting for precision electronics and medical trays.

翻砂鑄造鋁模Cast Aluminum Molds

先以木材、代木、石膏或電木製作原型模型，再進行翻砂鑄造成鋁模，最後經過拋光與建立風路。適合成本控管、造型較大或一般賣場包裝模具。 Created by sand casting aluminum from wood or tooling board patterns, followed by hand polishing and vent hole drilling. Cost-effective for large retail blisters.
