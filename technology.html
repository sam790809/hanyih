真空成型原理與技術解析Thermoforming Process & Mold Engineering

真空成型原理Thermoforming Process Principle

真空成型係將熱塑性塑膠板材或卷料加熱軟化後，將模具與材料間抽真空，利用大氣壓力使材料緊密服貼於模具表面，隨後經由風冷或水冷冷卻定型，最後吹氣脫模，即完成初步成型。 Thermoforming involves heating a thermoplastic sheet until pliable , then drawing a vacuum between the sheet and the mold so atmospheric pressure forces the sheet onto the mold surface. Once cooled by air or water, air is blown to release the finished part.

成型後需進行後加工及裁切，常見如：沖床沖壓（適用於立體造型邊緣）、雷射刀模沖切（裁切平面外框與蝴蝶洞）、熱封/高週波黏合、網版印刷與折邊加工。 Post-processing options include hydraulic press trimming, steel rule die cutting, heat/high-frequency sealing, silk-screen printing, and edge folding.

真空成型特性說明Technical Characteristics

  • 厚度變化Thickness Variation： 材料經拉伸後，各處厚度不均勻，拉伸高度越高之處壁厚越薄，最大厚度不超過原始板材厚度。 Sheet stretching causes non-uniform wall thickness; deeper draws result in thinner walls.
  • 轉角 R 角Corner Radius (R-Corner)： 受到材料覆蓋與拉伸限制，造型轉折處必然呈現 R 角，無法做到極致尖角。 Material stretch constraints necessitate rounded corners rather than sharp 90-degree angles.
  • 開發成本優勢Cost-Effective Tooling： 相較於射出成型模具，真空成型模具開發費用極低，特別適合中大量及客製化生產。 Tooling costs are significantly lower than injection molds, making it ideal for medium-to-large custom runs.

模具製造工藝Mold Tooling Technologies

CNC 精密切削鋁模CNC Machined Aluminum Molds

直接採用一整塊高品質鋁合金原料，透過 CNC 數值控制铣床直接切削成型。優點為尺寸精度極高、表面光滑、風路設計精細，適合高精度的電子 Tray 盤與醫療包裝模具。 Directly carved from solid high-grade aluminum blocks via CNC milling. Provides superior dimensional accuracy, smooth surface finish, and optimized vacuum venting for precision electronics and medical trays.

翻砂鑄造鋁模Cast Aluminum Molds

先以木材、代木、石膏或電木製作原型模型，再進行翻砂鑄造成鋁模，最後經過拋光與建立風路。適合成本控管、造型較大或一般賣場包裝模具。 Created by sand casting aluminum from wood or tooling board patterns, followed by hand polishing and vent hole drilling. Cost-effective for large retail blisters.

熱封模Heat-Sealed Molds

熱封模是用於熱塑性包裝材料（如泡殼對泡殼、泡殼與紙板、泡殼-紙-泡殼複合結構或其他特殊材質）封口的關鍵模具設備。 透過精確的溫度控制、均勻的壓力分佈與精準的機械行程，熱封模能夠在短時間內將包裝邊緣熔合，形成氣密、防潮且防篡改的高強度密封保護， 廣泛應用於各行各業。 A heat sealing mold is a critical tooling component utilized for sealing thermoplastic packaging materials, including blister-to-blister, blister-to-card, blister-card-blister sandwiches, and other multi-material combinations. By combining precise temperature control, uniform pressure distribution, and accurate mechanical stroke, the heat sealing mold fuses packaging edges within a short cycle time to create an airtight, moisture-proof, and tamper-evident high-strength seal, widely used across various industries.

高週波模High-Frequency Molds

高週波模是用於高週波塑膠熔接機的關鍵模具，專門針對熱塑性塑膠材料進行熱合。 透過高頻電磁場使塑膠分子產生高速極化摩擦發熱，在模具施壓下，讓材料在極短時間內從內部融化並緊密結合，形成氣密、 水密且強度超越材質本身的完美封口，廣泛應用於各行各業的塑膠製品加工。 A high-frequency mold is a critical tooling component utilized in high-frequency plastic welding machines specifically designed for the thermal fusion of thermoplastic materials. By applying a high-frequency electromagnetic field to induce rapid polar friction among plastic molecules, the material melts internally and bonds tightly under precise mold pressure within a fraction of a second, creating an airtight, waterproof, and exceptionally strong seal that exceeds the material's original strength, widely applied across various plastic processing industries.

治具Jigs and Fixtures

治具是用於固定和定位工件的專用工具，確保加工過程中的精確性和一致性。透過精確的設計和製造， 治具能夠有效提高生產效率、降低不良率，並確保產品品質的穩定性。材質方面廣泛採用鋁、電木、代木、EVA、塑膠等多種複合材料， 以滿足不同製程與產業的輕量化、耐磨損或絕緣需求。 A jig/fixture is a specialized tooling device used to secure and position workpieces, ensuring precision and consistency throughout the manufacturing process. Through meticulous design and fabrication, fixtures effectively boost production efficiency, reduce defect rates, and guarantee stable product quality. A wide range of composite materials—such as aluminum, Bakelite, tooling board (代木), EVA, and various engineering plastics—are utilized to meet specific lightweight, wear-resistant, or insulation requirements across different manufacturing processes and industries.
